# SSL in WordPress Studio

## Local HTTPS

Studio creates local domains with HTTPS so the development environment behaves more like a published site. This helps test secure cookies, redirects, and features that require a trusted origin.

## Trust the certificate

The operating system may ask for permission to trust Studio's local certificate authority. Follow the flow shown by the app and approve only certificates generated by the Studio installation on that machine.

## Troubleshooting

If the browser shows a warning, confirm that the certificate was accepted, restart Studio and the browser, and check that the system date is correct. On managed devices, installing the certificate may require IT support.

## Workshop use

Certificate issues must be resolved during preflight. The exercise does not reserve time for corporate device policies. The fallback is to demonstrate the site on the facilitator's machine and continue local editing without sharing credentials.
