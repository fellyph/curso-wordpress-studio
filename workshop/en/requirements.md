# requirements.md - AI event website in Faro

> Fictional teaching artifact created from the meeting notes. The team must review it before it guides design or implementation.

## 1. Context

- In-person artificial intelligence event in Faro, Portugal.
- Organized by the WordPress community but open to the wider public.
- Proposed date: October 24, 2026.
- Proposed venue: Hotel AP Eva Senses.
- Planning audience: 100 attendees; the brief also mentions serving 150 people, which remains to be confirmed.

## 2. Purpose

Bring artificial intelligence closer to Algarve businesses and professionals through practical content, accessible language, and examples grounded in local life. WordPress brings the organizing community together, but it is not a prerequisite for understanding or attending the event.

## 3. Priority audience

- Owners and teams from hotels, restaurants, and travel agencies.
- Small-business owners and independent professionals in southern Portugal.
- People curious about artificial intelligence, including those without technical or WordPress experience.
- The local WordPress community, speakers, volunteers, and sponsors.

## 4. Tone of voice

- **Welcoming:** explain without assuming technical knowledge.
- **Practical:** connect AI to recognizable local-business situations.
- **Clear:** prefer direct sentences and explain unavoidable terms.
- **Trustworthy:** distinguish possibilities, limits, and unconfirmed information.

## 5. Priority functional requirements

| Area | Requirement | Prototype acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Explain the proposition, date, venue, and intended audience | A person understands the event and finds the primary action in under one minute |
| Agenda | Show parallel Business and Tech tracks | Times, sessions, and breaks can be compared |
| Registration | Simulate ticket selection and confirmation | The flow ends without real payment or data |
| Participation | Provide speaker, volunteer, and sponsor forms | Each audience has a distinct path and fictional confirmation |
| Venue | Show address, availability, and an illustrative map | The venue is understood without additional contact |
| Contact | Receive general questions | The prototype form uses fictional data only |

## 6. AI assistance

- Suggest sessions from stated interests without hiding or deciding the agenda for the person.
- Summarize proposals and flag missing fields without accepting, rejecting, or ranking speakers.
- Answer questions from published content only and route gaps to a human contact.
- Prepare communication drafts that require human review before sending.

## 7. Boundaries and general criteria

- Use fictional names, images, prices, contact details, and submissions in the prototype.
- Identify every use of AI assistance and offer a non-AI route where applicable.
- Consider desktop and mobile from the first draft.
- Treat accessibility, readability, and contrast as design requirements.
- Do not implement payments, credentials, or production integrations in this phase.

## 8. Open questions

- Will operational capacity be 100 or 150 people?
- What is the final event name, and which brand assets will be available?
- What ticket tiers, prices, and refund policy will apply?
- What criteria and deadlines will govern talk submissions?
- Which AI-assisted capabilities belong in the first prototype?
